CSV Format Requirements
Required Columns:
  • Well ID: Unique identifier for each well
  • Latitude: Decimal degrees (e.g., 40.7589)
  • Longitude: Decimal degrees (e.g., -111.8883)
  • Date: Measurement date (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Water Table Elevation: Elevation in meters
Optional Columns:
  • Well Name: Descriptive name for the well
  • Well Depth: Total depth in meters
  • Ground Elevation: Surface elevation
  • Measurement Time: Time of measurement
  • Notes: Additional comments
Database Structure:

Your data will be mapped to two database tables:

Wells Table:
  • well_id, well_name, latitude, longitude
  • well_depth, elevation, created_date
Measurements Table:
  • measurement_id, well_id, date, time
  • water_table_elevation, notes, quality_flag
